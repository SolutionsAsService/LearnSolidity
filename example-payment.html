Payment for work

The employer deposits ether and credits workers; workers withdraw what they are owed. As written, it can promise more than it holds.

Run it yourself (a 882 KB download)

What happened when it ran

What it shows. Alice deposited 1 ETH, then credited Bob and Carol. After the second credit the contract had promised 2 ETH while holding 1 ETH, and both credits were accepted, because payWorker only checks that the balance covers this payment: address(this).balance >= _amount. Bob withdrew first and emptied the contract. Carol’s withdrawal reverted (reverted with no reason: the transfer had nothing to send) and the contract still records her as owed 1 ETH.

Terms

transaction
A signed message that changes the chain: deploys a contract, calls a function or sends ether. It costs gas.
revert
A transaction that stops and undoes everything it did. The sender still pays for the gas used up to that point.
event
A log line a contract emits. Apps listen for events; contracts cannot read them.

The fix

What it shows. The fix is one variable. PaymentForWorkFixed keeps totalOwed and requires balance >= totalOwed + _amount. The second credit is refused at the moment it is promised (“Not enough unpromised balance”), not when Carol tries to collect.

Every contract on this site was compiled with solc 0.8.28 and run in an in-memory EVM (EthereumJS, Cancun rules) when the site was built: 16 source files, 23 contracts, 494 transactions (96 listed on these pages; the rest are setup calls such as members joining, counted where they happen), 17 reverted on purpose.
